DocuGen – Privacy Policy

DocuGen is a personal tool that uploads documentary videos to its owner's own YouTube channels.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

DocuGen is a personal tool used by its owner to upload documentary videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the owner signs in to it.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, DocuGen asks for permission to upload videos to YouTube and to read the name of the channel it uploads to (the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly). It uses this access only to upload videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions that the owner has made, and to confirm which channel it is connected to.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored only on the owner's own computer. DocuGen has no server, does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and does not send any data to anyone other than Google's YouTube service.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account settings under Security, "Third-party apps with account access", and by deleting the stored tokens from the computer.

Use of Google user data

DocuGen's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
